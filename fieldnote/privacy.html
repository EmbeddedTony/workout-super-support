Fieldnote Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026
Publisher: Anthony Peytchev
Contact: contact@operationembedded.net

Your reports stay on your device

Fieldnote: Photo Job Reports does not collect or sell personal information through the app. It has no account system, analytics SDKs, advertisements, or cross-app tracking.

Photos and business information

Only photos you choose with Apple's system photo picker are copied into the app. Reports, captions, job addresses, client names, business details, and your logo are saved in the app's local storage. Fieldnote does not upload them to a publisher server. Your device's backup settings may include this local data.

Sharing PDFs

Exported PDFs include the photos and information you add. When you use the share sheet, the app or recipient you select receives that file and handles it under its own privacy practices. Review reports before sharing and include only information you have permission to use.

Apple purchases

Apple processes the optional non-consumable lifetime purchase and restoration. Fieldnote reads verified StoreKit transactions to provide access. The publisher does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Apple services are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Deletion and your choices

Delete reports from their editor or by touching and holding a report card. Remove your logo in Business and edit business details there. Uninstalling the app removes its local container. Device backups and previously shared or exported PDFs are managed separately. Deleting a report does not reset the free-report allowance. There is no publisher-hosted Fieldnote account to delete.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address, message, and attachments are processed through the support mailbox and its email provider to respond and maintain support records. Please do not send private client photos, passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information. Contact the publisher about a support-message deletion or retention request; applicable record-retention obligations may apply.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor connection information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages have no added analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or support forms.

Policy updates

Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Introducing collection, tracking, or a hosted report service would require updated disclosures.
