Fieldnote Terms of Use

Fieldnote uses Apple's standard end user license agreement.

Free reports and lifetime access

Three distinct real report exports are included at no charge. Re-exporting an existing report does not use another report. Labeled samples are free to explore. Deleting reports does not reset the allowance.

The optional non-consumable Fieldnote Lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited reports in this app. The final localized price appears in the App Store purchase sheet. This is a one-time purchase with no recurring subscription charge. Restore access using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Your content

Include only photos, logos, and client information you have permission to use and share. Review reports for accuracy. Keep separate copies of important reports; app data is stored locally and may be lost when the app is uninstalled.
