Fieldnote Support

Turn job photos into polished, branded PDF reports. Fieldnote is made for cleaners, landscapers, and property professionals.

For help, contact Anthony Peytchev at contact@operationembedded.net. Include your app version, iOS version, and a description of the problem. Please omit private client information and photos unless essential.

Create a report

In Reports, tap the plus button or Create a report. Enter job details, select up to 40 photos, and add captions and Before, After, or Detail labels. Use Move up to change the order. Changes are saved locally.

Add your branding

Use Business to select a logo and enter your business name, email, and phone. These details are included in exported reports.

Export and share

Open a report with at least one photo and a title, then tap Export PDF. In the preview, use Share to save to Files, send, AirDrop, or print. Keep separate copies of important reports.

Three free reports and Lifetime

You can export three distinct real reports for free. Re-exporting those reports is included, and labeled sample reports do not use the allowance. The optional Fieldnote Lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited reports with one payment and no subscription. The App Store shows the localized price before confirmation.

Restore an existing lifetime purchase using Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. If the store is unavailable, check your connection and try again. For a refund request, use Apple's Report a Problem.

Storage and deletion

Reports are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove them; export important reports first. Delete a report in its editor or by touching and holding its card. Removing a report does not return its free allowance.

Appearance

Settings includes System, Light, and Dark options. System follows your device appearance automatically.
