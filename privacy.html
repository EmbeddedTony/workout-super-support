Workout Super Privacy Policy

Updated October 22, 2026. Operator: Anthony Peytchev, developer of Workout Super. Contact: contact@operationembedded.net.

Your records stay on your device

Workout Super stores food logs, calories and macros, workout check-ins, weight entries, goals, written reflections, and any age, height, or equation inputs you provide in its private on-device storage. Reminder preferences and plan settings are also stored locally. We do not operate a server that receives these records, and do not use analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, or sell your information.

No account required

The app uses a private local profile. It does not request your name, email, or a login. A local identifier in the iOS Keychain connects the profile to its records. On upgrades from earlier versions, an existing saved identifier is retained to preserve records; previously saved names and email addresses are discarded. There is no app-managed account sync or cloud backup.

Camera, photos, and notifications

Camera access is optional and requested when you start barcode or nutrition-label scanning. Label images are processed on-device with Apple Vision and are not uploaded by the app. The system photo picker shares only the image you select with the app. Images are not saved in the app's record database. Reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. Written reflections are excluded from Lock Screen notifications by default; you may enable detailed content in Settings.

Food lookup services

When you search foods or look up a barcode, the entered text or code is sent to Open Food Facts. If the distributed build is configured for USDA FoodData Central, it may also send searches or barcode lookups there. Those services receive normal connection information, such as your IP address and app identification. We do not include your profile, weigh-ins, goals, or reflections in these requests. Manual food entry does not contact a food database.

Open Food Facts states that visitor IP/request logs are kept for three years for security, technical analysis, and popularity measurement. Food searches and barcode requests can therefore be associated with an IP address; we do not describe these requests as anonymous. We conservatively disclose search history, product interactions, and technical diagnostics for food-lookup functionality and provider analytics, potentially linked through connection information. These requests are not used by Workout Super for cross-app advertising tracking.

Read the Open Food Facts privacy policy and USDA privacy policy. Open Food Facts data is provided under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Purchases

Apple processes payments through StoreKit. The app receives verified product and transaction information to provide purchase benefits; it does not receive payment-card details. A purchase ledger is stored securely on this device to keep unused pass credits and prevent duplicate delivery. Subscriptions are tied to your App Store account and can be restored through Apple. Purchase records Apple is required to retain are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Retention, exports, and deletion

Local records are retained until you delete them. In Settings, choose “Delete profile and all local data” to delete the profile identifier, health and nutrition records, reflections, and plan settings. This does not cancel an App Store subscription or erase Apple's purchase history. The purchase ledger is retained to preserve paid credits and avoid duplicate transactions; it contains no nutrition, weight, or reflection records.

You can export your records from Settings to a destination you choose. Exports may include sensitive information. Exported copies and device backups are managed separately and are not erased by deleting a profile. Data may be included in backups managed by iOS under your device settings. Removing the app or losing the device can make records unrecoverable; the developer cannot retrieve them.

Your choices and questions

You may use manual entry, disable reminders in the app, revoke camera or notification access in iOS Settings, export records, or delete your profile. The app is intended for adults 18 and older. Privacy questions or requests may be sent to contact@operationembedded.net; do not send health records unless needed for a specific support request. If this policy changes, the updated date and policy will be made available in the app and on this page.

This website and support messages

This public privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor connection information, including IP addresses, for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, web forms, or cookies to these pages.

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and retain it only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the issue and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may request deletion by email. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or full health exports; ordinary support does not require them.
