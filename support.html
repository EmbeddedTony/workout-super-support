Workout Super Support

Contact Anthony Peytchev, developer of Workout Super, at contact@operationembedded.net. Include your app version, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Please omit private weight, food, and reflection details unless necessary.

Saving and exporting records

Records are stored on your iPhone. Use Settings → Export a copy of my records to save a readable JSON copy. The app currently exports but does not import records. It does not provide cloud sync. If your records cannot be opened, restart the app and contact support before reinstalling; deleting the app can remove records.

Health adjustments

Free health adjustments are available from Today, Settings, and the Extreme summary screen. Use them to change unsuitable nutrition targets, remove the workout requirement, and switch to Normal. Never restrict food or train through illness or symptoms to protect an app score. Targets, database values, and scans are estimates, not medical advice. Consult a qualified clinician before changing diet or exercise.

Purchases and Extreme

Premium monthly and yearly subscriptions include routine goal and plan flexibility, Sick Mode with flexible calories, and unlimited Extreme re-entry. Ending an Extreme attempt does not remove Premium benefits. One-time passes cover one attempt and are consumed when it starts. App Store purchases are optional; free logging and history remain available.

Use Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases for an existing subscription or an interrupted purchase. Finished consumable passes are not restored by Apple like a subscription; the local secure ledger preserves unused credits on the same device while available. Contact support if a purchased pass is missing before purchasing again.

To manage or cancel a subscription, use the app's Manage subscription button or App Store subscription settings. To request an Apple purchase refund, visit Apple's Report a Problem. Deleting a profile or app does not cancel a subscription.

Privacy and deletion

Settings → Delete profile and all local data removes local profile and health records. Exported copies and backups must be managed separately. Read our privacy policy for details.

Food search and scanning

Food lookup needs an internet connection and depends on the database provider. If a search or barcode lookup is unavailable, use manual food entry or review a nutrition-label scan. Always confirm the label and serving size before saving scanned or database values. Weigh-ins are entered manually; the app does not recognize scale photos.
