Find your learning path

Onboarding recommends a route based on your goals and experience. Include or skip the optional Arduino® track in You → Settings. Skipped lessons remain available in Explore. No Bit to Board online account is required.

Subscriptions and restoration

Eight starter lessons and all three Lab simulations are free. Bit to Board Pro unlocks the complete library through a monthly or yearly Apple subscription. Apple shows your price and any eligible trial before purchase. Hosted AI voice is not included.

Use Restore purchases in the paywall or You → Settings with the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Manage or cancel through Apple. Uninstalling Bit to Board does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Review every module

Each of the 29 modules ends with a four-question cumulative quiz after its lessons. Pass with three correct answers, review explanations, and earn 50 XP on your first pass. Retakes are available without an additional charge within your plan.

Hear each section

Device narration uses Apple speech and installed voices without an Bit to Board narration server. Check your volume and device voice availability if you cannot hear it. Optional AI narration requires your own trusted HTTPS server and voice-provider account. Bit to Board falls back to device speech if that request fails. Never place provider API keys in an app URL.

Progress and appearance

Your learning profile, progress, and preferences stay on this device. Bit to Board does not sync progress across devices. Reset progress in You → Settings clears lesson/activity progress; it does not clear your profile or every preference. You can also choose Light, Dark, or System appearance.

Independent courses and trademarks

Arduino® is a trademark of Arduino SA. Bit to Board is independently published and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Arduino.

Our hobby lessons cover projects with Arduino. Brand names identify the technologies discussed and do not imply certification or endorsement. See Arduino’s course trademark guidance.

Practice with real hardware

Lab activities simulate concepts. Bit to Board does not compile code or connect to boards. Independent projects require an external editor and suitable hardware or simulator. Check your board’s documentation and electrical limits before wiring.
