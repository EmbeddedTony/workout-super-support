Find your learning path

Onboarding recommends a route based on your goals and experience. Include or skip the optional Arduino track in You → Settings. Skipped lessons remain available in Explore. No Ember online account is required.

Subscriptions and restoration

Eight starter lessons and all three Lab simulations are free. Ember Pro unlocks the complete library through a monthly or yearly Apple subscription. Apple shows your price and any eligible trial before purchase. Hosted AI voice is not included.

Use Restore purchases in the paywall or You → Settings with the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Manage or cancel through Apple. Uninstalling Ember does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Hear each section

Device narration uses Apple speech and installed voices without an Ember narration server. Check your volume and device voice availability if you cannot hear it. Optional AI narration requires your own trusted HTTPS server and voice-provider account. Ember falls back to device speech if that request fails. Never place provider API keys in an app URL.

Progress and appearance

Your learning profile, progress, and preferences stay on this device. Ember does not sync progress across devices. Reset progress in You → Settings clears lesson/activity progress; it does not clear your profile or every preference. You can also choose Light, Dark, or System appearance.

Practice with real hardware

Lab activities simulate concepts. Ember does not compile code or connect to boards. Independent projects require an external editor and suitable hardware or simulator. Check your board’s documentation and electrical limits before wiring.

Privacy questions

Read Ember’s privacy policy for local storage, purchases, and optional narration. Email the contact above for questions or requests concerning information you send to support.
