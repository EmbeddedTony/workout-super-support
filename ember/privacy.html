Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026
Publisher: Anthony Peytchev
Contact: Contact@operationembedded.net

Your learning profile and progress stay on your device. Bit to Board has no online account, advertising, analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking.

This policy describes the Bit to Board iOS app, its optional narration feature, and contacting its publisher. Apple services, external websites, and any narration server you choose also have their own privacy practices.

Learning data on your device

Bit to Board stores your chosen name, learning goals and experience, Arduino® route selection, lesson completion, module quiz results, XP, activity history, daily goal, appearance, and voice preferences in the app’s local storage. These are used to personalize the learning path and show progress. Bit to Board does not transmit your learning profile or progress to a publisher account or cloud-sync service.

Apple subscriptions

Apple processes payments and manages Bit to Board Pro subscriptions. Bit to Board reads Apple-verified transactions and entitlements on your device to provide access. The publisher does not receive your payment-card details through Bit to Board. Purchases and Apple services are subject to Apple’s privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription through Apple.

Offline narration and optional server connection

Default narration plays bundled, AI-generated Kokoro Michael recordings offline. The app does not run or download a speech model. If a recording is unavailable, it uses Apple device speech. Offline playback does not upload narration text or audio. Bit to Board does not request microphone access or record your voice. Automatic narration can be changed using the Audio tag in a lesson or quiz, or in Settings; that preference stays on your device.

If you choose to configure an AI narration server, Bit to Board sends the current lesson narration text to that endpoint when you request narration. It does not include your learning profile or progress in that request. The server receives network connection information, such as your IP address, and may forward the lesson text to its voice provider.

The server connection is optional; bundled AI narration requires no server or voice-provider account and no separate voice payment. No publisher-hosted narration endpoint is bundled in this release, and hosted AI service is not included in Bit to Board Pro. Choose a server you trust and review its operator’s and voice provider’s policies, including logging and retention. Clearing the configured endpoint returns narration to bundled offline playback, with device speech as a fallback.

The separately supplied development-server software forwards lesson scripts to its configured voice provider and caches generated audio temporarily in process memory. A server operator may add hosting, logging, or storage with different practices; that operator is responsible for explaining them.

External references and this website

Opening a reference or an Apple management link uses an external service. Those services may process browser and network information under their own policies. This public privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor connection information, including IP addresses, for security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, or support forms to these pages.

Contacting support

If you email the publisher, your email address, message, and any attachments are processed through the support mailbox and its email provider to respond to your request and maintain records of the support conversation. This is separate from the app’s local learning data. Please do not send passwords, API keys, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

For questions about a support message, its retention, or a request to remove information you sent, contact Contact@operationembedded.net. Any applicable obligations to retain records or restrictions on deletion will be considered when responding to your request.

Your choices and deletion

  • Edit your learning, appearance, and voice preferences in You → Settings.
  • Reset progress in Settings to clear local lesson, module quiz, and activity progress. This does not clear your profile or all preferences.
  • Uninstall Bit to Board to remove its local app container. Apple-managed backups may retain data according to your backup settings.
  • Manage subscriptions separately through Apple. Resetting or uninstalling Bit to Board does not cancel a subscription.

There is no Bit to Board online account or publisher-held cloud learning profile to delete. A parent or guardian with questions about information a child sent to support can contact the publisher at the address above.

Policy updates

Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Adding publisher-hosted narration, analytics, or other data collection would require an updated policy and App Store privacy disclosures.
